What is Chess?

Chess is a strategic two-player board game played on an 8×8 board. It is a game of logic, planning, and concentration. Each player moves their pieces across the board and tries to checkmate the opponent's king. Chess is one of the most popular and oldest mind games in the world, with a history of more than 1500 years, originating in ancient India.

Chess Pieces

King

The most important piece on the board. If it falls, the game is over.

Queen

The most powerful piece, able to move in any direction across the board.

Knight

The only piece that can jump over other pieces on the board.

Greatest Chess Players in History

Throughout history, great players have emerged in the world of chess whose names are forever engraved in the game. From Garry Kasparov of Russia, who was world champion for years, to Magnus Carlsen of Norway, who broke many records with his modern and creative style. Bobby Fischer of the USA, José Raúl Capablanca of Cuba, and Alexander Alekhine of Russia are among other legends of the game.
